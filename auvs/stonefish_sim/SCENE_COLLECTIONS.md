# Pool scene collections

`data/pool_scene.blend` uses seven numbered groups. Collection colors distinguish
the groups in the Outliner. Every object belongs to one collection; parenting
continues to control vehicle motion and propeller animation.

```text
Scene Collection
├── 01 | Pool
│   ├── Pool | Basin                 pool tiles and lane band
│   ├── Pool | Deck and hall         coping, deck, building
│   └── Pool | Fixtures              rails, glass, underwater light housings
├── 02 | Water
│   ├── Water | Surface              active animated water body and its material
│   ├── Water | Particles            suspended particles
│   └── Water | Legacy               old volume, hidden in viewport and render
├── 03 | Vehicles
│   ├── AUV | Hydrus
│   ├── AUV | Proteus
│   ├── AUV | BlueROV2
│   └── AUV | Girona 500
│       Each vehicle contains:
│       ├── vehicle root empty       driven by the simulator or recorded poses
│       ├── <vehicle> | Body
│       ├── <vehicle> | Thrusters    rotor meshes and their mount empties
│       └── <vehicle> | Cameras and sensors
├── 04 | Mission
│   ├── Mission | Gates
│   ├── Mission | Markers and boards
│   ├── Mission | Poles
│   └── Mission | Bins
├── 05 | Cameras                     fixed pool cameras, Camera 1–5
├── 06 | Lighting
│   ├── Lighting | Environment       original sun, retained as a hidden backup
│   └── Lighting | Water             overhead reflection and caustics lights
└── 07 | Animation
    └── hydrus_takes                 paths generated by recorded runs
```

## Working with the groups

- Toggle a complete `AUV | ...` collection to show or hide one vehicle.
- Use `Body`, `Thrusters`, and `Cameras and sensors` when editing one part of a vehicle.
- Keep onboard cameras with their vehicle so hiding the vehicle hides its rig too.
- Add fixed viewing cameras to `05 | Cameras` and actual light objects to `06 | Lighting`.
- Put new mission targets in the matching subgroup under `04 | Mission`.
- Leave the old water volume hidden; the active surface already forms a closed water body.
- Recorded paths remain under `hydrus_takes`. The add-on recognizes that collection
  inside `07 | Animation` without adding a second link at the scene root.

## Simulation compatibility

The reorganization changes collection membership only. Existing object names,
parent relationships, transforms, materials, animation actions, custom export
properties, and the active camera are preserved. The hidden Girona collection
and the hidden legacy water volume retain their visibility settings.

The Hydrus camera uses the separate simulator-driven `camera` empty. It stays in
the Hydrus camera/sensor collection without being reparented to the vehicle.

## Sky environment

The surrounding sky is the World datablock `Pool | Daylight sky HDRI`. A World
is global to the scene, so it does not appear as an object in a collection.
The 4K partly cloudy Poly Haven HDRI is packed into the blend file.

In the Shader Editor, switch the context from Object to World:

- `Sky brightness` > Strength controls environment brightness (default 0.5).
- `Sky orientation` > Rotation Z turns the clouds and sun around the pool.

Eevee uses a 1024-resolution world reflection probe and extracts a shadow-casting
sun from the HDRI. `PoolSun` is hidden in viewport and render to avoid a second
sun direction. The old constant-color `World` datablock remains saved as a backup.
The overhead area lights remain available under `Lighting | Water`.

`Build Hydrus scene` reconstructs the starter scene from the XML and imports
objects into the currently active collection. After using that rebuild command,
move the new objects into the matching groups above. The saved pool scene already
contains the organized vehicle models and does not require a rebuild.
